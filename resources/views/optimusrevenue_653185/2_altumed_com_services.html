Leading the Way in Medical Billing Services Company

Partner with AltuMED for unmatched expertise, cutting-edge technology, and a client-centered approach to revenue cycle management services and medical billing solutions.

Our Mission

AltuMED is a healthcare innovator dedicated to empowering medical providers. We understand the complexities and challenges of modern healthcare billing. Our mission is to streamline your revenue cycle, maximize reimbursements, and allow you to focus on what matters most – delivering exceptional patient care.

Comprehensive Suite of Medical Billing Solutions

We offer a full spectrum of medical billing services company and RCM services to meet the unique needs of your practice:

  • Medical Billing and Coding  Services
  • Specialized Billing Services
  • Credentialing  Services
  • Business Intelligence & Analytics
Medical Billing and Coding  Services

Clean Claims: Our meticulous coding and claim submission processes ensure a 98% first-pass

clean claims rate, minimizing denials and maximizing reimbursements.


Accounts Receivable (A/R) Management: We actively manage your A/R to reduce outstanding balances by up to 30%, improving your cash flow.


Insurance Follow-up: Our dedicated team diligently follows up on all claims, ensuring timely

payments and resolving any issues that may arise.


Eligibility Verification: We verify patient insurance eligibility upfront, reducing claim denials and patient billing surprises.


HIPAA Compliance: Our HIPAA-trained staff ensures the privacy and security of your patient data at all times.


Prior and Retro Authorization: We tailor our prior authorization workflows to align with your practice's unique needs and preferences.

Specialized Billing Services

Laboratory Billing Services: Expert billing for laboratory procedures and tests.


Physician Billing Services: Tailored solutions for individual physicians and group practices.


MIPS Registry Services: Assistance with MIPS reporting and compliance.


Hospital Billing Services: Comprehensive billing for hospital-based services.

Credentialing  Services

Provider Credentialing: We handle the complex provider enrollment process with insurance payers.


Re-credentialing: We ensure your providers remain credentialed and eligible for reimbursement.


Business Intelligence & Analytics

Revenue Cycle Insights: Our advanced analytics provide deep insights into your financial performance, identifying areas for improvement and optimization.


Customized Reporting: We tailor reports to your specific needs, providing you with the

data you need to make informed business decisions.

FAQ(s)

What types of medical practices do you serve?

We serve a wide range of medical specialties, including primary care, cardiology, orthopedics, gastroenterology, dermatology, and more.

How do I get started with AltuMED?

Simply contact us for a free consultation. We'll assess your needs and tailor a solution to fit your practice.

Will AltuMED handle my existing outstanding claims?

Yes, we'll work diligently to collect on your existing A/R.

What kind of reporting can I expect?

We provide regular, detailed reports on your revenue cycle performance, including key metrics like clean claim rate, A/R aging, and collection ratios.

Is my data secure with AltuMED?

Absolutely. We adhere to the strictest HIPAA regulations to ensure the privacy and security of your patient data.
