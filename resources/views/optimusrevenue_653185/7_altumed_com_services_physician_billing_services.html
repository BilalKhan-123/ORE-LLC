At AltuMED, we redefine physician billing services. Being one of the best physician billing companies, we ease administrative burdens, manage complex A/R problems, and guarantee compliance with current requirements. Our staff knows how billing processes work for physicians thanks to extensive training. We handle every aspect of your revenue cycle management so that you may concentrate on quality patient care.

Comprehensive Physician Billing Services

Real-Time Revenue Management: Our team skillfully manages data processing, claims, denials, patient accounts, compliance processes, and audits. We fit everything into a robust system to ensure smooth, efficient RCM workflow and maximize reimbursements.

Error Reduction: We use advanced tools to avoid oversights in the revenue cycles with the aim of a zero-denial rate.

Revenue Management: We help reduce revenue loss through proper medical billing services provided to physicians and doctors. We strive to get you fair payment for your care.

Educational Support: We inform physicians about billing issues and their complications. We also help them adjust strategies to avoid these errors.

Tech Integration: We integrate the most advanced medical billing technology into your RCM operations. Not only does this automate the process, but it also helps locate and rectify errors.

Key Components of Our Physician Billing Services

Prior Authorization

We acquire approval from insurance companies and payers before the treatment is provided. The purpose is to prevent needless delays in payment processing and reimbursement.

e-Appointment & e-Registration

We bring e-appointment and e-registration solutions to streamline administrative tasks and improve efficiency. This front-end automation helps enhance patient satisfaction during their visits.

Insurance & Eligibility Check

We look into patient insurance details and verify their eligibility before the treatment. This proactive measure lowers the risk of claim denials or payment delays.

Charge Entry Support

We ensure that all the services rendered, diagnoses, and assigned codes are well documented. We take billing accuracy seriously to prevent revenue losses.

AR Follow-Up

Our team tracks and follows up on unpaid claims, rectifies reasons for denials and underpayments, and performs resubmissions. We ensure that you get every penny for your service.

Payment Posting

Our smart system updates the details of payments from insurers and patients into their accounts. It instantly adjusts account balances to ensure accuracy.

Patient Billing and Collections

We perform invoicing, set up payment schedules, and remind patients of outstanding balances, which improves your practice’s cash flow.

Reporting and Analytics

Our reports are detailed and help you understand your practice’s financials. These reports assist in strategic decision-making to increase revenue.
