Our Medical Billing Process

Patient Registration

Collection of data, such as patient’s full name, residential address, date of birth, emergency contacts, and insurance information.

Insurance Verification

Contacting the insurance provider to verify the insurance plan, services being offered, and pre-authorization requirements.

Documentation of the Patient's Encounter

Acquiring and entering the details of the patient’s condition, diagnosis, treatment procedure, and other information noted during the visit.

Medical Coding

Assigning medical codes to the procedures, i.e., ICD codes for diagnosis, CPT codes for treatments provided, and HCPCS codes for additional services.

Charge Entry

Assigning charges to all the services provided to the patient, including consultation, medications, and equipment.

Claim Submission

Preparing claims in the standard claim formats and submitting them electronically to the payers.

Appeals for Rejections

Examining the reason for denied or underpaid claims, fixing the errors, and re-sending those claims to recover complete reimbursements.

Payment Posting

Posting received payments to the patients’ accounts and sending accounts to collections for unpaid balances.

Setting Up Smooth Payment Options For Patients

As a growth-oriented medical billing company, we believe making payments convenient is crucial for accelerating patient collections. You want your patients to pay your bills as soon as possible. This is because patient bills do not require as much processing as insurance bills. All they need is a smooth payment option, and they will submit their fee at their earliest.

To accelerate patient collections, we have an advanced digital payment system. This system sends reminders through SMS or email, providing online payment links. The convenience it creates makes a majority of patients pay their fees immediately.
